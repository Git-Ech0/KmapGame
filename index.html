How to Play K-Maps in 4 Simple Rules

1. The Main Goal:
Circle every 1 using the fewest and largest circles possible.
2. Allowed Circle Sizes:
Circles must strictly have 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 cells (powers of 2). You can never circle 3, 5, or 6 cells!
3. Rectangles Only (Never Diagonals):
Circles must be horizontal lines, vertical lines, or solid rectangles.
4. Edges Connect (Pac-Man Rules!):
The board wraps around! The far-left column connects to the far-right column, and the top row connects to the bottom row.
5. Wildcards (X):
'X' means "Don't Care". You can include an X in a circle if it helps make your circle twice as big, but you don't have to circle it!
6. Never circle a 0!
